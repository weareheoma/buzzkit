# Buzzkit
English and Swahili news page. GitHub rebuilds `index.html` every 3 hours from RSS feeds.
- Add your own stories in `posts.txt`.
- Hide a feed story by adding its link to `hide.txt`.
- Add or remove news sources in `build.py` (the FEEDS list near the top).
- In `build.py`, paste your WhatsApp Channel link into `CHANNEL_URL` to show the "Join" box, and your email into `CONTACT_EMAIL` for the footer.
- In `posts.txt`, add `pinned: yes` to a story to make it the top story ("Morning read").
